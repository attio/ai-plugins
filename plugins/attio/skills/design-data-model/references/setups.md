# How businesses usually set up Attio

These are reference points, not rules. Use them to check a draft, or to tell the user "this is how businesses like yours usually set it up", then adapt to what they tell you. Infer the type of business from the evidence if there's enough. Otherwise ask.

| Business                       | Signals                                                       | Usually                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sales-led software or services | "Request a demo", no public prices, enterprise customer logos | People, Companies and Deals, and sometimes Products and Line items, Contracts or Invoices, if the team manages them in Attio. Software with account or usage data to sync also gets Users and Workspaces.                                      |
| Product-led software           | A "Get started" button, a free plan, usage-based pricing      | People, Companies and Deals, plus Users and Workspaces, with Champion as a link from Deals to People, and often a Customer Success list on Workspaces.                                                                                         |
| Partner or reseller-led        | A partner program, reseller or MSP language                   | A Partnerships object, one record per partner company or person, with its stage, tier and manager. Deals a partner brings in link to its partnership.                                                                                          |
| Agencies                       | Case studies, "Our work" pages, services rather than pricing  | Clients are companies, with Deals, and a Projects object is common.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Investors, such as VC          | A portfolio page, an investment thesis                        | An Investments object linked to Companies, with its own stage from sourcing to invested, so the portfolio is a view of it. Some use Deals instead. Add LPs, Funds and Commitments (one per LP per fund) if they raise their own fund in Attio. |
| Marketplaces                   | Separate pages for the supply and demand sides                | Deals for the businesses they sell to. Each side is people or companies, marked with a select. A Matching object to pair the two sides only if matching happens in Attio.                                                                      |
| Early-stage startups           |                                                               | People, Companies and Deals, plus a Startup Fundraising list if they're raising.                                                                                                                                                               |
| Recruiting and staffing        | Open roles for clients, candidate pages                       | Candidates are people and clients are companies. A Roles object is common, and an Applications object when candidates apply to several roles.                                                                                                  |

When a business fits several of these, model the main one in full, and add only what the others need.

What the user tells you and what their workspace already holds beat what you infer from their website. If the user and their workspace disagree, ask which is right.
