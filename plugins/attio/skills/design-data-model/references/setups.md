# How businesses usually set up Attio

These are reference points, not rules. Use them to check a draft, or to tell the user "this is how businesses like yours usually set it up", then adapt to what they tell you. People and Companies are always on.

| Business                                | Usually                                                                                                                       | Deal stages                                                               |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sales-led software or services          | Deals. Products and line items if the team builds quotes in Attio, and sometimes contracts or invoices.                       | Discovery → Demo → Proposal → Negotiation → Won · Lost                    |
| Software with product sign-ups          | Deals, Users and Workspaces, and often a Customer Success list on Workspaces.                                                 | Trial started → Activated → Sales qualified → Demo → Won · Lost           |
| Partner or reseller-led                 | Deals, with partners as companies and a Referred by link on Deals. A Partner program list if partners go through onboarding.  | Partner sourced → Co-sell approved → Joint demo → Quote sent → Won · Lost |
| Agencies and professional services      | Deals, with clients as companies. A Projects object is common.                                                                | Brief received → Scoping → Proposal → Statement of work → Won · Lost      |
| Investors, such as VC or private equity | A Portfolio list on Companies for deal flow. Funds and LPs objects only if they raise their own fund in Attio.                | In the Portfolio list, not Deals                                          |
| Marketplaces                            | Deals for partnerships with businesses on either side. A matching object only if matching happens in Attio, not another tool. | The stages of the side the team sells to                                  |
| Early-stage startups                    | Deals, plus a Startup Fundraising list.                                                                                       | As sales-led                                                              |
| Recruiting and staffing                 | A Recruiting list on People. Deals only if they sell to client companies.                                                     | As sales-led, if they sell to clients                                     |

## Signals on a website

| Signal                                                                           | Points to                                           |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| "Get started" or "Sign up free", a free plan, usage-based pricing                | Product sign-ups: Users and Workspaces              |
| "Request a demo" or "Talk to sales", no public prices, enterprise customer logos | Sales-led: Deals, maybe products and line items     |
| A partner program, or reseller or agency pages                                   | Partners as companies, maybe a Partner program list |
| Separate pages for buyers and sellers, or for two kinds of customer              | A marketplace                                       |
| Pricing per seat or per plan                                                     | Products only if the team quotes them in Attio      |

When a business shows several signals, pick the one that brings in most of its revenue as the main setup and add only what the others need, rather than modelling each one in full.
