# How businesses usually set up Attio

These are reference points, not rules. Use them to check a draft, or to tell the user "this is how businesses like yours usually set it up", then adapt to what they tell you. Infer the business from the evidence if there's enough, otherwise ask.

| Business                       | Tells                                                         | Usually                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sales-led software or services | "Request a demo", no public prices, enterprise customer logos | People, Companies and Deals, often with Products and Line items, and sometimes Contracts or Invoices, if the team works them in Attio.                                                            |
| Product-led software           | A "Get started" button, a free plan, usage-based pricing      | Adds Users and Workspaces, with Champion as a link to People, and often a Customer Success list on Workspaces.                                                                                    |
| Partner or reseller-led        | A partner program, reseller or MSP language                   | Partner organizations are companies with a tier select. Partner deals and market development fund requests can justify their own objects.                                                         |
| Agencies                       |                                                               | Similar to sales-led. Clients stay as companies, and a Projects object is common.                                                                                                                 |
| Investors, such as VC          |                                                               | The widest range. Some keep it simple with People, Companies and Deals or a deal-flow list. Others add LPs, Funds or Events objects. Common lists are Portfolio, VC Network and Founders Network. |
| Marketplaces                   | Separate pages for the supply and demand sides                | Deals for partnerships with businesses. A Matching object to pair the two sides only if matching happens in Attio. At high volume it usually happens in another tool.                             |
| Early-stage startups           |                                                               | People, Companies and Deals, plus a Startup Fundraising list.                                                                                                                                     |
| Recruiting and staffing        |                                                               | People and Companies, plus a Recruiting list.                                                                                                                                                     |

When a business shows several of these, commit to the main one, add what the others need on top, and don't model each one in full.

## Weigh the evidence

- The user's own words beat what you infer from their website. That means anything they tell you, their existing deal attributes, and their call notes, emails and descriptions of how the team works. When sources disagree, ask which is right rather than picking one.
- Useful signals on a website: the main button's wording, named customers and case studies, the pricing model and plans, partner pages, "For X" pages, and the job titles of the buyers it speaks to.
- Useful signals from the user: the things and pipelines they describe, "we want to track X", and anything that happens again and again.
- Treat gaps as questions for the user, never as facts.
