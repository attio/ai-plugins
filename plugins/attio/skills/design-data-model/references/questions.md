# Questions to ask

The user knows how their team works, and a confidently wrong model costs far more than a short question. When a gap would change what gets built, ask before writing anything.

- Ask only when the answer changes which objects, relationships or lists you create. Don't ask what the evidence already answers clearly, or about details that are easy to change later, such as option or attribute names.
- Ask up to 3 or 4 at a time, each one standing on its own, each with 2 to 4 options. Put the recommended option first, and say why in a line. Use plain, friendly words, not Attio terms the user may not know.
- If the user doesn't know or has no preference, use the recommended option and list it as an assumption in the draft.
- Never write while a question that changes the structure is still open.

| When it's unclear                | Ask                                                                                                                                                            | Recommend                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| How they win customers           | Do customers mostly sign up themselves, come through sales demos, come through partners or resellers, or meet through your marketplace?                        | What their website's main button suggests. If it's mixed, sales-led.                                             |
| Who they sell to                 | Do you sell to small businesses, mid-sized companies, large enterprises, or one industry?                                                                      | Mid-sized businesses, with no objects specific to an industry.                                                   |
| A company or a person            | Is it an organization or an individual? Does it need its own details, owner or history?                                                                        | Companies or People with attributes, rather than a new object.                                                   |
| What partners do                 | Do partners refer deals, sell alongside you, or resell? What do you track about them?                                                                          | Partners as companies with a tier select, and nothing more yet.                                                  |
| Their role and main use of Attio | What do you do, and will you mainly use Attio to win new customers, look after existing ones, raise money, hire, or invest?                                    | Winning new customers, the most common use. The answer decides which lists fit (`lists.md`).                     |
| Product data                     | Does your product have usage, account or billing data you'd want to see in Attio? If so, do you track each person who uses it, each customer account, or both? | No, leaving Users and Workspaces out. If yes, both, plus a Pricing model select.                                 |
| Quotes, contracts and invoices   | When you send a customer a quote or invoice, where do you put it together: in Attio, or in another tool, such as your billing or quoting software?             | Summary attributes on the deal or company, such as ARR or Contract end date, unless they'll build them in Attio. |
| Investors                        | Do you invest in companies, raise your own fund, or both?                                                                                                      | A Portfolio list, adding LPs and Funds only if they manage their own fundraising in Attio.                       |
