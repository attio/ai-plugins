# Questions to ask

Ask only when the answer changes which objects, relationships, lists or pipelines you create, and the user hasn't already said. Use plain, friendly words, not Attio terms the user may not know. Put the recommended option first, with why in a line.

| When it's unclear                          | Ask                                                                                                                                                | Recommend                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| How they win customers                     | Do customers mostly sign up themselves, talk to your sales team, come through partners or resellers, or meet through your marketplace?             | What their website's main button suggests. If it's mixed, sales.                                                 |
| What they'll use Attio for                 | Will you mainly use Attio to win new customers, look after existing ones, raise money, hire, or invest?                                            | Winning new customers, the most common use. The answer decides which lists fit.                                  |
| Who they sell to                           | Do you sell to small businesses, mid-sized companies, large enterprises, or one industry?                                                          | Mid-sized companies, with nothing specific to an industry.                                                       |
| Whether something is a company or a person | Is it an organization or an individual? Does it need its own details, owner or history?                                                            | The standard object, Companies or People, with attributes.                                                       |
| Product data                               | Does your product have usage, account or billing data you'd want to see in Attio?                                                                  | No, leaving Users and Workspaces out.                                                                            |
| Users or accounts                          | Do you want to track each person who uses your product, each customer account, or both?                                                            | Both, Users and Workspaces.                                                                                      |
| Pipelines                                  | Which kinds of deals does your team work: new business, renewals, partner deals?                                                                   | One new business pipeline.                                                                                       |
| Quotes, contracts and invoices             | When you send a customer a quote or invoice, where do you put it together: in Attio, or in another tool, such as your billing or quoting software? | Summary attributes on the deal or company, such as ARR or Contract end date, unless they'll build them in Attio. |
| Partners                                   | Do partners refer deals, sell alongside you, or resell? Do they go through stages, such as onboarding?                                             | Partners as companies with a Relationship type, and a Partner program list only if they go through stages.       |
| Investors                                  | Do you invest in companies, raise your own fund, or both?                                                                                          | A Portfolio list, adding Funds and LPs only if they manage their own fundraising in Attio.                       |
