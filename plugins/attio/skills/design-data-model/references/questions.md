# Questions to ask

- Ask only when the answer changes which objects, relationships or lists you create. Don't ask what the evidence already answers clearly, or about details the user can easily change in Attio later, such as attribute names.
- Ask at most 4 at a time, and use the recommended option for any others. Make each one stand on its own, with 2 to 4 options. Put the recommended option first, and say why in a line. Use plain, friendly words, and explain any Attio term the user may not know.
- If the user doesn't know or has no preference, use the recommended option and list it as an assumption in the draft.

| When it's unclear                                                       | Ask                                                                                                                                     | Recommend                                                                                                                         |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| How they win customers                                                  | Do customers mostly sign up themselves, come through sales demos, come through partners or resellers, or meet through your marketplace? | What their website's main button suggests. If it's mixed, sales-led.                                                              |
| Whether something they track, such as clients, is a company or a person | Are your clients organizations or individuals?                                                                                          | Companies or People with attributes, rather than a new object.                                                                    |
| What partners do                                                        | Do you manage each partner over time, with an owner and a stage, or only need to mark which companies are partners?                     | A Partnerships object linked to the partner company or person. If they only want to label a few companies, a select on Companies. |
| What they'll use Attio for                                              | Will you mainly use Attio to win new customers, look after existing ones, raise money or invest, or hire?                               | Winning new customers, the most common use. The answer decides which lists fit (`lists.md`).                                      |
| Product data                                                            | Does your product have usage, account or billing data you'd want to see in Attio?                                                       | Yes for a product-led business. Otherwise no, leaving Users and Workspaces out.                                                   |
| Users or accounts, for a product-led business                           | Do you track each person who uses your product, each customer account, or both?                                                         | Both, Users and Workspaces.                                                                                                       |
| Pipelines                                                               | Which kinds of deals does your team work: new business, renewals, partner deals?                                                        | One new business pipeline.                                                                                                        |
| Client work                                                             | Do you track client projects in Attio, or in a project tool?                                                                            | Their project tool, with a Projects object only if they'll manage projects in Attio.                                              |
| Quotes, contracts and invoices                                          | Do you put together quotes, contracts or invoices in Attio, or in another tool, such as billing, quoting or e-signature software?       | Summary attributes on the deal or company, such as ARR or Contract end date, unless they manage them in Attio.                    |
| Investors                                                               | Do you invest in companies, raise your own fund, or both?                                                                               | An Investments object linked to Companies, adding LPs, Funds and Commitments only if they manage their own fundraising in Attio.  |
