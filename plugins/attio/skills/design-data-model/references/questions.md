# Questions to ask

When a gap would change what gets built, ask before drafting.

- Ask only when the answer changes which objects, relationships or lists you create. Don't ask what the evidence already answers clearly, or about details the user can easily change in Attio later, such as attribute names.
- Ask at most 4 at a time, each one standing on its own, each with 2 to 4 options. Put the recommended option first, and say why in a line. Use plain, friendly words, and explain any Attio term the user may not know.
- If the user doesn't know or has no preference, use the recommended option and list it as an assumption in the draft.
- Never write to the workspace while a question that changes the structure is still open.

| When it's unclear                             | Ask                                                                                                                                                | Recommend                                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| How they win customers                        | Do customers mostly sign up themselves, come through sales demos, come through partners or resellers, or meet through your marketplace?            | What their website's main button suggests. If it's mixed, sales-led.                                                                                                  |
| Who they sell to                              | Do you sell to small businesses, mid-sized companies, large enterprises, or one industry?                                                          | Mid-sized businesses, with no objects specific to an industry.                                                                                                        |
| A company or a person                         | Is it an organization or an individual?                                                                                                            | Companies or People with attributes, rather than a new object.                                                                                                        |
| What partners do                              | Do partners refer deals, sell alongside you, or resell?                                                                                            | If partnerships are a core part of the business, a Partnerships object linked to Companies. Otherwise partners as companies with a tier select, and nothing more yet. |
| What they'll use Attio for                    | Will you mainly use Attio to win new customers, look after existing ones, raise money or invest, or hire?                                          | Winning new customers, the most common use. The answer decides which lists fit (`lists.md`).                                                                          |
| Product data                                  | Does your product have usage, account or billing data you'd want to see in Attio?                                                                  | No, leaving Users and Workspaces out.                                                                                                                                 |
| Users or accounts, for a product-led business | Do you track each person who uses your product, each customer account, or both?                                                                    | Both, Users and Workspaces, plus a Pricing model select.                                                                                                              |
| Pipelines                                     | Which kinds of deals does your team work: new business, renewals, partner deals?                                                                   | One new business pipeline.                                                                                                                                            |
| Quotes, contracts and invoices                | When you send a customer a quote or invoice, where do you put it together: in Attio, or in another tool, such as your billing or quoting software? | Summary attributes on the deal or company, such as ARR or Contract end date, unless they build quotes and invoices in Attio.                                          |
| Investors                                     | Do you invest in companies, raise your own fund, or both?                                                                                          | An Investments object linked to Companies, adding LPs and Funds only if they manage their own fundraising in Attio.                                                   |
