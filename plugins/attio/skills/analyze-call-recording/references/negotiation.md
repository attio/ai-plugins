# Negotiation

The purpose of a negotiation is to agree on terms and a date for the decision.

## Sections

- **Commercials:** price, quantity, term, and any discounts asked for or offered, exactly as stated.
- **Objections:** each objection, and whether it was resolved.
- **Path to signature:** the remaining steps (legal, security, procurement), with owners and dates.

## Watch for

- Concessions given without anything in return.
- No clear decision maker or signer.
- No date for the decision.
