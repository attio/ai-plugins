# Evaluation check-in

The purpose of an evaluation check-in is to confirm that the trial, pilot or review is on track to a decision.

## Sections

- **Success criteria:** what the buyer set out to prove, and the status of each item.
- **Blockers:** what is stuck, why, and who owns the fix.
- **Engagement:** who on the buyer's side is involved or using the product, and any new stakeholders.
- **Timeline:** the decision date and whether it moved.

## Watch for

- No agreed success criteria. This is the biggest risk in an evaluation, so put it first under Gaps and risks.
- A single contact doing all the evaluating.
- A decision date that slipped or was never set.
