# Demo

The purpose of a demo is to show that the product solves the buyer's problems, and to learn what matters most to them.

## Sections

- **What landed:** moments the buyer engaged with, such as follow-up questions, links to their own use case, or "can it also do...".
- **What fell flat:** confusion, silence, a change of subject, or "we wouldn't use that".
- **Concerns:** questions about integrations, security, migration, or comparisons with other tools ("in X we can..."). Note whether each was answered.

## Watch for

- A feature tour that is not tied to the problems the buyer described.
- Many feature questions with no link to a use case.
- Little buyer participation.
- No agreed next step.
