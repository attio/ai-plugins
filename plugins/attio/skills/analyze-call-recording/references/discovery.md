# Discovery

The purpose of discovery is to learn whether the buyer has a real problem worth solving, and who owns it.

## Sections

- **Situation:** who they are, their current process and tools, and who is involved day to day.
- **Problems:** specific pains in the buyer's words, with the impact in time, money or risk where they stated it.
- **Why now:** the event or threshold that started this conversation now rather than six months ago.
- **Decision:** who decides, who else is involved, and the process and timeline they described.
- **Budget:** what they said about budget, current spend or price expectations.

## Watch for

- Problems described only in general terms, with no example or impact.
- No reason for urgency.
- Only one contact, with no mention of who else is involved.
- The seller pitching before they understood the problem.
